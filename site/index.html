It started with borrowed phones.

Filmmaking began as a childhood obsession for four best friends. We shot backyard mysteries on whatever phone we could borrow. Weekends went to scripting twists and cutting scenes together.

Jahz Studios is what that lifelong habit turned into: a formal studio, with the same four people behind every frame.

Four friends. One studio.

We grew up making films together. Each of us now runs one part of the craft.

What a Jahz thriller refuses to do.

No cheap scares.

Typical thrillers lean on sudden loud noises. We build slow-burn psychological dread inside everyday homes.

No distant worlds.

Instead of unrelatable characters in faraway places, we tell stories from Kenyan youth perspectives, with global appeal.

No easy endings.

Formulaic plots get guessed early. Ours are built on layered betrayals and twists that reframe the entire story.

The audience is already there: 72% of Gen-Z streaming viewers rank mystery and suspense among their top genres.
